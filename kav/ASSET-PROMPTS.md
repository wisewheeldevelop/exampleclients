# KAV — Image generation prompt set

כל ארבעת הנכסים נוצרו באמצעות כלי `imagegen` המובנה במצב ברירת המחדל. קובצי PNG המקוריים וקובצי JPG דחוסים לשימוש באתר נמצאים ב־`assets/images/`.

## `kav-hero`

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: premium ecommerce homepage hero, 16:10 landscape
Primary request: Original photorealistic campaign image for KAV, a premium Israeli urban electric mobility concept. One sophisticated matte-black folding fat-tire electric bicycle and one refined matte-black standing electric kick scooter parked together beside a modern protected bicycle lane in Tel Aviv at early morning.
Scene/backdrop: contemporary pale-stone Tel Aviv architecture, Mediterranean plants, limestone sidewalk, distant sea atmosphere without landmarks or tourist clichés.
Composition/framing: wide low camera angle, products on the right half, calm negative space on the left for Hebrew UI copy, full wheels visible.
Lighting/mood: soft golden Israeli morning light, quiet and aspirational.
Constraints: no text, lettering, logos, trademarks, watermark, readable plates, impossible parts, extra vehicles or people.
```
